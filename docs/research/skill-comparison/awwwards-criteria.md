# What Awwwards actually scores — primary sources only

Fetched 2026-11-08 from awwwards.com. Anything not stated by Awwwards is marked as such.

## Weights (awwwards.com/about-evaluation/)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Design    | 40% |
| Usability | 30% |
| Creativity| 20% |
| Content   | 10% |

Design + Usability = 70% of the score. **Creativity is only 20%.** A site that is
novel but hard to use loses more than a site that is conventional and flawless.
Awwwards does not publish a definition of each criterion — the weights are all
they state.

## Thresholds

- **Honorable Mention**: jury score 6.5 or higher, AND a user score meeting the
  same threshold (FAQs).
- **Developer Award**: only SOTD winners go to the developer jury; above 7 gets
  the award.
- **Site of the Day**: no published numeric cutoff — highest-scoring sites win.
  Can win early with a high jury score plus 10+ PRO user votes.
- **Site of the Month**: the eight highest-scoring sites of the month.
- A site is eligible for SOTD only for up to 3 months after approval.

## Nomination mechanics

- Approved sites go to a minimum of 18 jury members.
- The 3 scores furthest from the average are discarded — so one hostile or one
  generous juror cannot swing it. Consistency across 18 people beats a polarising
  idea.
- Voting runs 5 days. Only validated PRO user votes count.
- Awwwards does not publish a minimum score for nomination itself.

## Submission rules (FAQs)

- **No pre-made templates.** Design and development must be the submitter's own.
- Main thumbnail 1600x1200 px.
- Approval is manual, up to a week.
- No edits once under review.
- $65 standard submission.

## Not stated by Awwwards

They publish **no** requirement on responsiveness, accessibility, performance,
or motion. Those are not rules — but they land inside Usability (30%), which is
the second-heaviest criterion and the one most often lost on mobile.

## What this means for our three builds

1. Craft carries more weight than concept. 70% is design + usability.
2. The mobile build is not a port, it is half the score.
3. Content is 10% and ours is already strong — do not let a redesign dilute it.
4. 18 jurors with outliers trimmed rewards the site that nobody can fault, not
   the one two people love.
